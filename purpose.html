Our purpose

The “third place” for active, curious people—neither home nor work, but a welcoming space to belong.

Why a third place?

Modern life can be isolating—screens, schedules, and costs make community hard. We aim to counter that with an accessible, movement-first space where people meet, play, and grow together.

What we’re solving

  • Limited safe spaces for teens and young adults to be active after school/work.
  • Cost and intimidation barriers to trying new movement disciplines.
  • Lack of positive, mixed-age community experiences.

How we’ll do it

  • Inclusive programming: parkour, group training, ninja, bouldering, and events.
  • Beginner pathways and clear progressions.
  • Community-led culture with regular meetups and volunteer opportunities.
